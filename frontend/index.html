HOW THE VERDICT WAS REACHED

ANGLE CONFORMITY — How close are the corners to the ideal samosa shape? The three corners are compared with the chosen ideal angles: 70° at the tip and 55° at each base corner. Closer angles receive a higher score.

SIDE CONFORMITY — Are the sides in the right proportions? The measured side lengths are compared with the proportions expected from the ideal triangle.

SYMMETRY — Do the two sloping sides agree with each other? The closer they are in length, the more symmetrical the specimen is.

FINAL SSI — So... how geometrically samosa is this samosa? The final score combines angle conformity (35%), side conformity (35%), and symmetry (30%).
